-Make an App for Android cellphone. User: an elderly over 70 years old, just finished hips and knees joints replacement surgery for 3 months, so need to train the leg muscles to recover the leg strength required for basic stand-up and sit-down movement. 
Functions: 
-Remind the user to routinely conduct training to enhance muscle strength and joint rotating range, including 1.彈力帶彎腿 2.阻力伸腿 3. 阻力騎腳踏車.The app can use the cell phone’s alarm function to remind the user, with a encouraging message like ‘要做訓練囉，要有耐心，一定會進步，恢復行動自如，加油!’
-Provide input modules for the user to daily select what training to do , record the duration of ‘彈力帶彎腿, reps and sets and weight loading of ‘阻力伸腿’, duration and weight level of ‘騎腳踏車’
-Provide dashboard and statistic of the records.
-Can share the records to others.
